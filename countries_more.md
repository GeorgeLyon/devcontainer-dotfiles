# 20 More Countries, Capitals & Fun Facts

| Country | Capital | Fun Fact |
|---------|---------|----------|
| Portugal | Lisbon | One of the oldest cities in Western Europe, predating Rome by centuries. |
| Germany | Berlin | Has more bridges than Venice, around 1,700 of them. |
| Sweden | Stockholm | Spread across 14 islands, earning it the name "Venice of the North." |
| Finland | Helsinki | Has more saunas than cars. |
| Poland | Warsaw | Its Old Town was rebuilt from paintings after WWII destruction. |
| Austria | Vienna | Repeatedly ranked the world's most livable city. |
| Ireland | Dublin | Its name comes from the Irish "Dubh Linn," meaning "black pool." |
| Denmark | Copenhagen | Has more bicycles than residents. |
| South Korea | Seoul | Blends ancient palaces with some of the fastest internet on Earth. |
| China | Beijing | Home to the Forbidden City and its nearly 9,000 rooms. |
| Vietnam | Hanoi | Over 1,000 years old, famous for its charming Old Quarter. |
| Malaysia | Kuala Lumpur | Crowned by the Petronas Towers, once the world's tallest buildings. |
| Chile | Santiago | Sits dramatically against the snow-capped Andes. |
| Colombia | Bogota | One of the highest capitals in the world at about 2,640 m. |
| Morocco | Rabat | Its name means "stronghold of victory." |
| New Zealand | Wellington | The world's southernmost capital, nicknamed "Windy Welly." |
| Hungary | Budapest | Two cities, Buda and Pest, joined across the Danube in 1873. |
| Czech Republic | Prague | Home to the largest ancient castle complex in the world. |
| Switzerland | Bern | Only the de facto capital; Switzerland never officially named one. |
| Belgium | Brussels | The unofficial capital of the European Union. |
